Vision-Language and Agentic AI Modeling in Image-guided Theranostics

A focused interdisciplinary forum exploring how vision-language models, multimodal foundation models and agentic AI can advance image-guided diagnosis, intervention and theranostic systems.

Workshop attendance: DICTA workshops are free for contributors, based on the organising correspondence supplied for this site.

Why this workshop

Bridging AI innovation with clinically meaningful imaging workflows

Recent progress in computer vision has transformed medical imaging, while large language models, vision-language models and agentic AI are opening new possibilities for multimodal reasoning and workflow automation.

This workshop brings together researchers, engineers, clinicians and industry experts to discuss methods, systems, benchmarks and translational pathways for the next generation of image-guided theranostics.

Workshop scope

Topics of interest

From foundational methods to real-world image-guided intervention.

01

Vision-language & LLMs

Vision-language and large-language AI modelling for image-guided medical interventions.

02

Agentic AI & RAG

Agentic AI, retrieval-augmented generation and decision support for complex clinical workflows.

03

Foundation models

Foundation models for medical imaging, image-guided robotics and translational applications.

04

Multimodal learning

Endoscopy, bronchoscopy, endovascular and other intraluminal procedures.

05

Scene understanding

Anatomical segmentation, recognition, surgical scene parsing and semantic mapping.

06

Workflow intelligence

Procedure phase, workflow and action recognition; skill, intent and operator modelling.

07

Navigation & spatial AI

Image-guided navigation, depth estimation, 3D reconstruction and spatial reasoning.

08

Human–AI collaboration

Clinically grounded interaction, decision support and responsible deployment.
